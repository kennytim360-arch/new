  ⚠ Sharpe Ratio: 0.5-1.0 shows value even if <1.0

RED FLAGS:
  ✗ Maximum Drawdown > Benchmark (strategy failing to protect)
  ✗ Downside Capture > 80% (not providing downside protection)
  ✗ Beta > 1.2 (taking MORE risk than market)
  ✗ Negative Alpha with Beta >1.0 (worse than just buying SPY)
